VYORA WEBSITE — INDIA HOURLY WORK BOOKING PREVIEW

FILES
All website files are directly in the ZIP root. There is no enclosing project folder and no assets folder.

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Internet access is needed for Google Fonts and remote decorative photos in the stylesheet. The hero video and poster are included locally.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Premium black and champagne-gold responsive VYORA landing page.
- India-only state and city signup fields.
- Client and Worker demo signup/login role selection.
- Eight service groups with 50+ service types.
- INR price explorer with 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8-hour rates, plus indicative weekly, fortnightly and monthly package calculations.
- Fixed booking windows: 8 AM–2 PM (6 hours), 2 PM–8 PM (6 hours), and 8 AM–4 PM (8 hours).
- Client demo booking form with category, service, state, city, address, date, duration, slot and estimated service charge.
- Worker demo profile and payout-request panel.
- Admin preview with percentage/fixed commission setting, booking completion and manual payout status.
- Muted autoplaying, looping hero video and local poster image.

IMPORTANT — NOT PRODUCTION READY
This is a front-end prototype only. Signup/login, profiles, bookings, commission, earnings and payout records are simulated in the browser and are not securely persisted. No real worker availability is checked, no payment is collected and no payout is transferred. The rates are suggested starting prices, not guaranteed market quotes. Package estimates assume an 8-hour daily shift, 6 working days per week; confirm weekly off and exact duties with each worker.

Before public launch, connect a secure backend/database, real authentication, worker onboarding and admin permissions, compliant identity verification provider, qualification/licence checks for regulated work, payment and refund workflows, secure storage, notifications, audit logs, dispute handling and legally reviewed terms/privacy policies. Aadhaar OTP is not implemented; use only an authorised verification channel/provider and do not collect Aadhaar details in this static website.

Service boundaries: only lawful, consensual work. Patient care, nursing/medical tasks, medicine handling, driving, spa/wellness and other regulated services require applicable credentials, licences, prescriptions and safeguards. Shopping purchases, transport, tolls, materials and equipment are separate from worker service charges.
