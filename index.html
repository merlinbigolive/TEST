Safety always.

Workers are subject to identity and profile checks before public approval. Patient care, driving, medicine handling and other regulated tasks require relevant qualifications, licences or prescriptions. All work must be lawful, consensual and clearly described.

GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers.
Better bookings.

Are shopping items included in the service charge?＋

No. Grocery, vegetable, fruit, meat, fish and medicine purchases are paid separately by the customer. The worker's service fee is shown separately.

What booking durations are available?＋

1 to 5 hours, the 6-hour morning or evening slot, the 8-hour full-day slot, 1 week, 2 weeks and 1 month.

Can I book a patient caregiver or driver?＋

Yes, subject to city availability and verification of relevant training, qualifications or a valid driving licence. Medical procedures are limited to appropriately qualified professionals.

Are these live prices and worker listings?＋

This ZIP is a front-end preview. Prices are suggested starting rates and profiles/availability are demo-only until a secure backend and verified worker network are connected.

Does VYORA transfer worker earnings automatically?＋

No automatic payout is configured in this preview. The intended workflow is worker payout request, admin review and manual transfer with a transaction record.
